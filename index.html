How ClipMaster AI works

1. Import: choose a video file from your device.

2. Find a moment: the local scanner samples frames and suggests visually active sections. You can also set start/end manually.

3. Caption: add your own subtitle lines and timing. Captions are drawn onto the exported clip.

4. Export: play and record the selected section in your browser. WebM is the most broadly supported output in this no-server prototype.

Honest limitation: real semantic AI highlight ranking and automatic transcription require a model running on your device or a configured AI service. This prototype does not pretend those are already connected.

Video links: direct media-file URLs may work if the host allows browser playback. YouTube URLs are recognized, but processing them requires a secure backend and an authorized source.
